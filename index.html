Private by design

Your photos, videos, contacts and files never leave your device. Juno has no account, no ads and no tracking. You stay in control: nothing is deleted until you confirm it, and deleted photos remain in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

Juno Pro

Juno is free to download. Juno Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription that unlocks unlimited swipes, with a free trial for new subscribers. You can see the price in the app before you subscribe and cancel any time in your Apple ID settings.
