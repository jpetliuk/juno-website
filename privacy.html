Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Juno helps you clean up your photos, videos, contacts and files by swiping. This page explains what Juno does with your information. The short version: your content stays on your device, and Juno has no account, no ads and no tracking.

What Juno accesses on your device

  • Photos and videos. Juno reads your photo library so you can review items and choose which to keep or delete. To find duplicate photos it makes tiny grayscale copies of your photos on your device and compares them there.
  • Contacts. Juno reads your contacts to find duplicates and empty entries, and edits, merges or deletes the ones you choose.
  • Files. Juno reads the folder you pick and deletes only the files you choose.

You decide what to delete. Nothing is deleted until you confirm it. Photos you delete stay in the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app for 30 days.

What leaves your device

Your photos, videos, contacts and files are never uploaded, sent or shared. Juno has no server and no account.

What Juno stores on your device

Juno keeps a small amount of data locally so the app works between launches: your settings, the items you chose to keep (so Juno does not show them again), totals such as the space you have freed, and a small cache that speeds up duplicate detection. This data never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Subscriptions and purchases

Juno Pro is a subscription. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID; Juno never sees your card or payment details.

To confirm whether a subscription is active, Juno uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier for the app installation, your purchase and subscription information, and technical details such as your IP address and device type. It does not receive your name, your email address, or any of your photos, contacts or files. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy.

What Juno does not do

  • No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

Juno does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can revoke Juno's access to your photos and contacts at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone. Deleting Juno removes everything it stored on your device. You can cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings. To ask about the anonymous purchase record RevenueCat holds, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
